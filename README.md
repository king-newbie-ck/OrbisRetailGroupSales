# OrbisRetailGroupSales
This page gives a one-glance summary of Orbis Retail Group's overall sales performance. It combines six KPI cards on the left (Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin, Total Quantity, Average Order Value) with a set of supporting visuals on the right showing how those numbers break down.

Total Sales by Month — a column chart showing monthly sales across the year, used to spot which months perform best.
Total Sales by Region — a bar chart ranking the five regions (North, South, East, West, Central) by revenue.
Total Sales by ProductName — a donut chart breaking sales down by individual product.
Total Sales by Year — a line chart comparing 2024 vs 2025 performance.
Sum of Discount, Total Sales and Total Orders — a scatter chart meant to show the relationship between discounting and sales volume.
Total Sales by Day — a column chart showing sales spread across days of the month.
Quarter — a slicer letting users filter the whole page by Qtr 1–4.
Total Sales by Category — a treemap showing revenue split across Office, Sports, Electronics, Home, and Fashion.
Regional summary table — a table listing each region's Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin, with a grand total row.
